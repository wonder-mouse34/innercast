## Notes

- **Left out on purpose:** Pokémon (Bulbapedia, 64,264 articles) and The Witcher
  (12,336). Both wikis are mostly about the games, not the TV series. Star Wars
  (Wookieepedia, 228k) was left out because it's a whole franchise.
- **Couldn't be queried:** Detective Conan World (API returned no stats),
  The 100 (thehundred.fandom.com is gone), and Heroes (heroeswiki.com no longer resolves).
- Ranking by article count favours long-running soaps, procedurals and kids' shows
  (NCIS, Coronation Street, PAW Patrol) over prestige dramas with fewer episodes.
- A few wikis have huge page/edit counts from user blogs and image pages rather than
  articles (e.g. Glee, The Vampire Diaries, Degrassi), so "articles" is the fairer measure.
- Alternate Simpsons wiki: https://simpsons.fandom.com (24,880 articles).
  Game of Thrones also has "A Wiki of Ice and Fire" (https://awoiaf.westeros.org), which
  covers the books in depth.
- Every wiki above runs MediaWiki, so all expose the same API at `<URL>/api.php`
  (exceptions: `https://simpsonswiki.com/w/api.php`, `https://en.battlestarwiki.org/w/api.php`).
- To refresh the numbers: `python3 fetch_wiki_stats.py`
