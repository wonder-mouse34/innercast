## Caught between a friend and the boss

**User:** My friend got into a fight with my boss and now I am not sure who's side to take. Feels horrible.


> **What I'm hearing:** It is incredibly draining to be caught in the middle of a conflict between two people who occupy different corners of your life. When a friend’s anger clashes with your boss’s authority, that tension can feel like a heavy weight on your own shoulders, forcing you to question where your loyalties should lie.

>
